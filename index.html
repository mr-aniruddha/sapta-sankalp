Methodology & sources

What this site is

An observational tracker of publicly available price series related to import-heavy categories mentioned in the seven appeals. The site presents data and does not interpret whether the appeals have "worked." Readers are encouraged to form their own views.

How instruments were chosen

For each appeal, the most direct listed proxy was selected: sector indices where one exists (e.g. Nifty FMCG for Swadeshi consumption), individual listed companies where a sector index does not exist (e.g. fertilizer, cooking oil), and commodity benchmarks for raw imports (Brent crude, gold). Where two or more instruments are reasonable, both are shown — primary instruments as solid lines, alternates as dashed.

Rebasing

Every series is rebased to 100 on 10 May 2026 (the speech date). This makes instruments of very different price scales directly comparable on a single axis. Tooltips show both the rebased index value and the raw closing price.

Data sources

Market data (equities, indices, commodities, currencies): Yahoo Finance, fetched daily. India CPI: FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), series INDCPIALLMINMEI, updated monthly. Retail fuel prices (petrol, diesel, domestic LPG): public sources, carried forward when no revision is announced.

Caveats

Some instruments carry signal noise: IndiGo operates international routes too, so fewer outbound trips by Indians may not straightforwardly hurt it. Sector indices have composition risk (Nifty Tourism is heavily weighted toward airlines). These are flagged in tooltips where relevant.
